# consumer-protection/ — Consumer authorities

VVTAT (LT), PTAC (LV), TTJA (EE), plus the ODR platform referrals.

Handles:

- Consumer complaint referrals and sweep participation (coordinated EU
  sweeps — e.g., dark patterns, fake reviews: our exposure map lives in
  `platform-regulation/consumer-law/`).
- Unfair-terms challenges to our legal texts: the challenged clause is
  frozen for analysis; outcome feeds a legal-text version bump and
  `_clause-library.md` learning.
- ADR/ODR outcomes: logged here with the decision summary (non-privileged
  unless counsel is engaged — then cross-linked to the matter).

Convention: per-authority subfolders as volume warrants; single-file
entries until then.
