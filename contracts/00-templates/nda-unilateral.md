## Deal-sheet defaults

| Parameter | Default | Concession limit |
|-----------|---------|------------------|
| Direction | JOL → recipient only | keep unilateral |
| Term | 2 years | 3 years max |
| Confidentiality period | 3 years | trade secrets: indefinite |
| Permitted recipients | named individuals, need-to-know | no class expansion |
| Governing law | per CL-10 | per CL-11 |

## Structure

1. Parties & effective date
2. Confidential information definition (JOL-side only)
3. Standard of care (≥ own, not less than reasonable)
4. No sublicensing, no reverse engineering of disclosed materials
5. Compelled disclosure: prompt notice to JOL, minimum necessary
6. Return/destruction on request (certified within 15 days)
7. No license, no warranty, no obligation to contract
8. Breach remedies: injunctive relief + damages (CL-02 carve-outs)
9. Term, survival, governing law, notices

## Linkages

- Prefer mutual NDA where both sides disclose; unilateral only when JOL is
  the sole discloser. Do not sign counterparty unilateral NDAs where JOL
  receives information without reciprocal protection.
