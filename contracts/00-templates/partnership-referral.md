## Deal-sheet defaults

| Parameter | Default | Concession limit |
|-----------|---------|------------------|
| Exclusivity | none | territory exclusivity only w/ GC + minimum commitments |
| Economics | referral fee or commission per schedule; GC+finance sign-off | |
| IP | CL-40 co-created content; CL-42 brand usage license, revocable | partners never own JOL marks |
| Data | DPA required if partner touches buyer/seller personal data | |
| Term | 12 months, rolling, 60-day notice (CL-30) | |

## Structure

1. Parties, purpose, non-exclusivity baseline
2. Referral/channel mechanics; tracking & attribution
3. Economics: fee schedule, audit of referral data, payment (CL-50)
4. Obligations: compliance with marketplace legal texts (link current
   versions from `legal-texts/`), sacred-goods sensitivity standards
5. Brand usage: limited license, style guide, revocation on termination
6. Data protection & DPA linkage
7. IP (CL-40/CL-41/CL-42)
8. Representations: authority, no conflicts
9. Term, termination, effects (CL-30/CL-31)
10. Liability caps (CL-01/CL-02), governing law (CL-10)

## Watch items

- Church/institutional partners: verify legal capacity of the signing
  entity (diocese vs parish vs foundation — different legal persons).
- Referral data authenticity ties to Omnibus review-authenticity duties
  (`platform-regulation/consumer-law/`).
