## Deal-sheet defaults

| Parameter | Default | Concession limit |
|-----------|---------|------------------|
| Purpose scope | named project only | no broadening |
| Term | 2 years | 3 years max |
| Confidentiality period | 3 years from disclosure | trade secrets: indefinite |
| Governing law | per CL-10 | per CL-11 |
| Residuals clause | rejected | no-concede |

## Structure

1. Parties & effective date
2. Definition of confidential information (written/oral marking rules)
3. Exclusions (public, independently developed, prior possession, third-party right)
4. Obligations: need-to-know, protection standard ≥ own information
5. No license / no obligation to proceed
6. Compelled disclosure procedure (notice + cooperation)
7. Return/destruction on termination (certified)
8. No warranty; no IP grant
9. Injunctive relief acknowledgement
10. Term, survival, governing law, notices
