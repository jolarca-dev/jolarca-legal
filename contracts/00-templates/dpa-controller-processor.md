## 1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 Terms not defined herein have the meanings given in Regulation (EU)
2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR") and, where
applicable, Commission Implementing Regulation 2021/914 (Standard
Contractual Clauses, "SCCs").

1.2 Key terms:

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| "Personal Data" | Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person |
| "Processing" | Any operation performed on Personal Data (Art. 4(2) GDPR) |

## 2. Role and scope of processing

2.1 JOL is the **controller** of the Personal Data shared with the
Processor. The Processor processes Personal Data only on JOL's behalf
and on JOL's documented instructions (Art. 28(3)(a) GDPR).

2.2 The nature, purpose, categories of Personal Data, and categories of
Data Subjects are described in **Annex I** (Processing Description).

2.3 The duration of processing is the term of the Principal Agreement
plus the period necessary for return or deletion of Personal Data
following termination.

---

## 3. Obligations of the Processor

### 3.1 Instructions

The Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented
instructions from JOL, including instructions regarding transfers. If
the Processor is required by EU or Member State law to process Personal
Data, it shall inform JOL before processing unless the law prohibits
such disclosure.

### 3.2 Confidentiality

The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process Personal
Data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations (Art.
28(3)(b) GDPR).

### 3.3 Security

The Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate TOMs as described
in **Annex II**, taking into account the state of the art, costs of
implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of
processing (Art. 28(3)(c) and Art. 32 GDPR).

### 3.4 Sub-processors

(a) JOL authorises the Sub-processors listed in **Annex III** (the
"Sub-processor List").

(b) The Processor shall not engage additional Sub-processors without
JOL's prior written consent. For **general authorisation**, the
Processor shall notify JOL at least **30 days** before engaging a new
Sub-processor. JOL may object on reasonable data-protection grounds.

(c) The Processor shall flow down all data-protection obligations to
each Sub-processor by written contract, including obligations
equivalent to this DPA (Art. 28(4) GDPR).

(d) The Processor remains fully liable to JOL for the performance of
each Sub-processor's obligations.

### 3.5 Data Subject rights

The Processor shall assist JOL by appropriate technical and
organisational measures to respond to Data Subject requests under
Art. 15–22 GDPR. The Processor shall:

(a) Notify JOL without undue delay of any Data Subject request received;
(b) Not respond directly to Data Subjects unless authorised by JOL;
(c) Bear the cost of assistance unless the request results from the
Processor's breach or unauthorised processing.

### 3.6 Breach notification

The Processor shall notify JOL of a Breach **without undue delay** and
in any event within **48 hours** of becoming aware. The notification
shall include:

(a) Nature of the Breach, categories and approximate numbers of Data
Subjects and Personal Data records affected;
(b) Likely consequences;
(c) Measures taken or proposed to address the Breach and mitigate its
effects;
(d) Name and contact details of the Processor's data protection
officer or equivalent contact.

The Processor shall cooperate with JOL and provide additional
information as requested. The Processor shall document all Breaches
regardless of notification.

### 3.7 Data protection impact assessment

The Processor shall assist JOL with data protection impact assessments
(Art. 35 GDPR) and prior consultation with supervisory authorities
(Art. 36 GDPR) to the extent the processing requires it.

### 3.8 Records and audit

(a) The Processor shall maintain a written record of all processing
activities carried out on behalf of JOL (Art. 30(2) GDPR).

(b) JOL may audit the Processor's compliance with this DPA:
- **Annual audit:** Once per calendar year, with 30 days' prior notice.
- **Incident-triggered audit:** At any time following a Breach or
  reasonable suspicion of non-compliance.
- **Format:** On-site inspection, remote audit, or review of
  third-party audit reports (ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II) where
  equivalent in scope.

(c) The Processor shall cooperate fully with audits and provide all
necessary information and access.

## 4. International transfers

4.1 The Processor shall not transfer Personal Data to a country outside
the EU/EEA without JOL's prior written consent and a valid transfer
mechanism.

4.2 Where transfers occur, the Parties shall enter into SCCs (Commission
Implementing Regulation 2021/914) with the appropriate module (Module 2
for controller-to-processor transfers). The SCCs form part of this DPA.

4.3 Where SCCs are used, the Processor shall cooperate with JOL's
Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA) and implement any supplementary
measures identified.

4.4 Where the Processor relies on an adequacy decision, it shall provide
evidence of the decision's continued validity.

---

## 5. End of services

5.1 Upon termination or expiry of the Principal Agreement (or earlier,
at JOL's written request), the Processor shall, at JOL's choice:

(a) **Return** all Personal Data in a commonly used machine-readable
format within **30 days**; or
(b) **Delete** all Personal Data within **30 days** and certify
destruction in writing.

5.2 The Processor shall delete existing copies of Personal Data in
accordance with applicable law, except to the extent required by law to
retain copies (in which case the Processor shall inform JOL of the
legal basis for retention).

5.3 The Processor shall certify completion of return or deletion in
writing within 10 days of completion.

---

## 6. Liability

6.1 Each Party's liability under this DPA is subject to the liability
provisions of the Principal Agreement, with the following carve-outs:

(a) **Uncapped liability** applies to:
- Breach of confidentiality obligations;
- Infringement of data protection obligations;
- IP infringement;
- Wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

(b) **Liability cap** for other breaches: 12 months' fees paid or
payable under the Principal Agreement in the 12 months preceding the
event giving rise to the claim.

6.2 Nothing in this DPA limits either Party's liability under Art. 82
GDPR to Data Subjects or supervisory authorities.

---

## 7. Term and termination

7.1 This DPA enters into force on the date of last signature and
remains in effect for the duration of the Principal Agreement.

7.2 Either Party may terminate this DPA if:
(a) The other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy within
30 days of notice;
(b) The other Party becomes insolvent or enters liquidation.

7.3 Termination does not affect the Processor's obligations under
Sections 3.5 (Data Subject rights), 3.6 (Breach notification), 3.8
(Records), and 5 (End of services), which survive termination.

## Annex II — Technical and organisational measures (TOMs)

The Processor shall implement and maintain the following minimum TOMs:

| Category | Measures |
|----------|----------|
| **Access control** | Role-based access, least privilege, MFA for administrative access |
| **Encryption** | TLS 1.2+ in transit; AES-256 at rest for Personal Data |
| **Pseudonymisation** | Where feasible, pseudonymise Personal Data |
| **Physical security** | ISO 27001 certified data centres; visitor logs |
| **Network security** | Firewalls, IDS/IPS, network segmentation |
| **Logging and monitoring** | Access logs retained ≥ 90 days; security event monitoring |
| **Backup and recovery** | Daily backups; RTO ≤ 24 hours; RPO ≤ 1 hour |
| **Vulnerability management** | Regular scanning; patching within 30 days of critical CVE |
| **Personnel** | Background checks; annual security training; NDAs |
| **Incident response** | Documented IR plan; tested annually |
| **Sub-processor oversight** | Due diligence before engagement; annual review |

The Processor shall provide evidence of compliance (ISO 27001
certificate, SOC 2 Type II report, or equivalent) upon request.
