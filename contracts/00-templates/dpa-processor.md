# Data Processing Agreement — JOL as processor

**GDPR Art. 28 compliant template (rare — institutional customers)**

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** approved
**Paper:** JOL standard form

---

> **IMPORTANT — ROLE ANALYSIS GATE:**
>
> This template is used ONLY where JOL acts as **processor** on behalf of
> an institutional customer (controller). This is the exception, not the
> rule. Before using this template:
>
> 1. A role analysis memo must be filed in `opinions/` confirming that
>    JOL is genuinely a processor (not a controller or joint controller).
> 2. General Counsel approval must be recorded.
> 3. The sub-processing chain must be verified (JOL's own processors
>    listed and acceptable to the institutional controller).
>
> Most marketplace flows make JOL a controller or independent controller.
> The processor role applies only to institutional arrangements (e.g.,
> diocese-type white-label deployments where the institution determines
> the purposes and means of processing for its users).

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 Terms not defined herein have the meanings given in Regulation (EU)
2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR").

1.2 Key terms:

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| "Personal Data" | Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person |
| "Processing" | Any operation performed on Personal Data (Art. 4(2) GDPR) |
| "Data Subject" | The natural person whose Personal Data is Processed |
| "Breach" | Personal data breach as defined in Art. 4(12) GDPR |
| "Sub-processor" | Any processor engaged by JOL to process Personal Data on behalf of Controller |
| "TOMs" | Technical and organisational measures |

## 2. Role and scope of processing

2.1 The Controller determines the purposes and means of processing. JOL
processes Personal Data only on the Controller's **documented, written
instructions** (Art. 28(3)(a) GDPR).

2.2 The nature, purpose, categories of Personal Data, and categories of
Data Subjects are described in **Annex I** (Processing Description).

2.3 No verbal instructions are valid. All instructions must be in
writing (email or designated instruction portal). JOL is not liable for
actions taken in good faith on instructions reasonably believed to be
from the Controller's authorised representative.

2.4 The duration of processing is the term of the Principal Agreement
plus the period necessary for return or deletion of Personal Data
following termination.

---

## 3. Obligations of JOL (Processor)

### 3.1 Instructions

JOL shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the
Controller, except where required by EU or Member State law. Where such
legal obligation applies, JOL shall inform the Controller before
processing unless the law prohibits such disclosure.

### 3.2 Confidentiality

JOL shall ensure that its personnel authorised to process Personal Data
are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations (Art. 28(3)(b)
GDPR).

### 3.3 Security

JOL shall implement and maintain the TOMs described in **Annex II**,
consistent with JOL's ISO 27001 certification and SOC 2 Type II
commitments.

### 3.4 Sub-processors

(a) JOL's current Sub-processors are listed in **Annex III**.

(b) JOL shall notify the Controller at least **30 days** before engaging
a new Sub-processor. The Controller may object on reasonable
data-protection grounds within 30 days of notification. If the
Controller objects and the Parties cannot agree within 30 days, the
Controller may terminate the affected Services.

(c) JOL shall flow down all data-protection obligations to each
Sub-processor by written contract.

### 3.5 Data Subject rights

JOL shall assist the Controller by appropriate technical and
organisational measures to respond to Data Subject requests under
Art. 15–22 GDPR. JOL shall:

(a) Notify the Controller without undue delay of any Data Subject
request received directly by JOL;
(b) Not respond directly to Data Subjects unless authorised by the
Controller;
(c) Provide reasonable assistance (including access to stored data) at
the Controller's documented request.

### 3.6 Breach notification

JOL shall notify the Controller of a Breach **without undue delay** and
in any event within **72 hours** of becoming aware. The notification
shall include:

(a) Nature of the Breach, categories and approximate numbers of Data
Subjects and Personal Data records affected;
(b) Likely consequences;
(c) Measures taken or proposed to address the Breach and mitigate its
effects;
(d) Name and contact details of JOL's DPO.

JOL shall cooperate with the Controller and provide additional
information as reasonably requested.

### 3.7 Assistance

JOL shall assist the Controller with:

(a) Data protection impact assessments (Art. 35 GDPR);
(b) Prior consultation with supervisory authorities (Art. 36 GDPR);
(c) Demonstrating compliance with Art. 32 (security) obligations.

### 3.8 Records and audit

(a) JOL shall maintain a written record of processing activities
undertaken on behalf of the Controller (Art. 30(2) GDPR).

(b) The Controller may audit JOL's compliance with this DPA:
- **Annual audit:** Once per calendar year, with 30 days' prior notice.
- **Incident-triggered audit:** At any time following a Breach.
- **Scope limitation:** Audits must not grant unrestricted access to
  other clients' data or JOL's infrastructure beyond the scope of the
  Services provided to the Controller.
- **Format:** On-site inspection, remote audit, or review of
  third-party audit reports (ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II) where equivalent
  in scope.

## 4. International transfers

4.1 JOL shall not transfer Personal Data to a country outside the EU/EEA
without the Controller's prior written consent and a valid transfer
mechanism.

4.2 Where transfers occur, the Parties shall enter into SCCs
(Commission Implementing Regulation 2021/914) with the appropriate
module.

4.3 JOL shall cooperate with the Controller's Transfer Impact Assessment
and implement supplementary measures identified.

---

## 5. End of services

5.1 Upon termination or expiry of the Principal Agreement (or earlier,
at the Controller's written request), JOL shall, at the Controller's
choice:

(a) **Return** all Personal Data in a commonly used machine-readable
format within **30 days**; or
(b) **Delete** all Personal Data within **30 days** and certify
destruction in writing.

5.2 JOL shall delete existing copies except to the extent required by
law, informing the Controller of the legal basis for retention.

5.3 JOL shall certify completion of return or deletion in writing within
10 days.

---

## 6. Liability

6.1 JOL's liability under this DPA is subject to the liability
provisions of the Principal Agreement, with the following carve-outs:

(a) **Uncapped liability** applies to:
- Breach of confidentiality obligations;
- Infringement of data protection obligations by JOL;
- IP infringement;
- Wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

(b) **Liability cap** for other breaches: 12 months' fees paid or
payable under the Principal Agreement in the 12 months preceding the
event.

6.2 Nothing in this DPA limits either Party's liability under Art. 82
GDPR to Data Subjects or supervisory authorities.

6.3 JOL is not liable for processing that results from the Controller's
own instructions, unless such processing violates mandatory GDPR
provisions that apply to processors irrespective of instructions.

---

## 7. Term and termination

7.1 This DPA enters into force on the date of last signature and
remains in effect for the duration of the Principal Agreement.

7.2 Either Party may terminate this DPA if:
(a) The other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy within
30 days of notice;
(b) The other Party becomes insolvent or enters liquidation.

7.3 Termination does not affect obligations under Sections 3.5, 3.6,
3.8, and 5, which survive termination.
