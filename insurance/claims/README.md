|----------|--------|-------------|---------|-------------|--------|-------------|--------------|

Rules:

- Every claim row records the **notice date** and the policy window it
  had to meet — this is audited at renewal.
- Correspondence files per claim: `YYYY-MM-DD-<party>-<direction>.md`;
  reservation-of-rights and coverage-denial letters flagged for GC
  same-day.
- Links to `disputes/` matter ids only — no privileged substance here.
