# closed/ — Archived matters & outcome register

Closed matter folders move here intact (history preserved). The
**outcome register** below is the durable index:

| Matter | Opened | Closed | Counterparty | Category | Outcome | Cost tier | Lesson (non-privileged) |
|--------|--------|--------|--------------|----------|---------|-----------|-------------------------|

Rules:

- Outcomes feed `jolarca-compliance/risk-register` in non-privileged
  phrasing only — no strategy, no settlement amounts outside GC channel.
- Retention per `docs/retention-schedule.md`; limitation-period checks
  (reopening risk) are noted at closure.
- Reopening a closed matter: move back to `active/` with a note; the
  closed entry gets a "reopened" marker, never deleted.
