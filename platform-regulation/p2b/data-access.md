# P2B data access disclosure — Art. 9

**Regulation (EU) 2019/1150, Art. 9 (access to data).**

## Legal position

Terms must describe: (a) whether professional users can access personal
or other data they provide or generated through the service, (b) under
what conditions, (c) whether they can access data in aggregated form,
(d) technical access means (API/export).

## Our disclosure (mirrored in `legal-texts/seller-agreement/` § Data access)

1. **Own data access** — sellers can retrieve their listing data, order
   data, and performance metrics; formats and API/export surface listed
   per product capability.
2. **Buyer personal data** — access limited to what fulfillment requires
   (name, delivery address, contact); reuse restrictions stated
   explicitly (no marketing reuse without independent lawful basis —
   cross-reference `jol-m-compliance/lawful-basis`).
3. **Aggregated data** — what category-level analytics we expose
   (currently: sales dashboards; extend deliberately).
4. **Portability at exit** — export window during offboarding
   (see seller-agreement § Term and exit).

## Change control

Data-access capability changes in product must be reflected here and in
the seller-agreement version that ships with them.
