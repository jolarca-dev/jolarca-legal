# watches/ — Horizon scanning

One memo per instrument; every memo ends with a **review date** (expired
reviews surface at the quarterly GC check-in).

Expected memos:

- `ai-act.md` — Reg. (EU) 2024/1689 applicability to our AI-assisted
  features (chatbot classification, recommendation/ranking as AI system,
  moderation automation); timeline of obligations per risk class.
- `dac7.md` — platform seller-income reporting (DAC7 / 2021/514) —
  when seller payout data must be reported to LT tax authorities.
- `data-act.md` — Reg. (EU) 2023/2854 where IoT-connected goods appear
  in the catalog.
- `accessibility.md` — European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU)
  2019/882) obligations on the storefront from 28 June 2025.
- `digital-fairness.md` — Commission digital-fairness initiative
  (dark patterns, influencer practices) as it evolves.

## Memo template

1. Instrument + status (proposal / adopted / applicable-from).
2. Trigger analysis: do we fall in scope, and when.
3. Impact on legal-texts / product specs / contracts.
4. Action items with owners.
5. **Review date** (max 6 months out).
