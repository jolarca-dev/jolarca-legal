# DSA transparency reports — Art. 15 / Art. 24

**Regulation (EU) 2022/2065: Art. 15 (hosting-service transparency
reporting), Art. 24 (enhanced reporting incl. Art. 24(2) public reports
for online platforms).**

Cadence: **semi-annual**, driven by `transparency-report-data.yml`
(Jan 15 / Jul 15 data-collection tasks).

## Required content (our profile: online platform, not VLOP)

- Moderation measures: removals/demotion/suspensions, split by
  own-initiative vs. notice-driven, by category of illegality and by
  contractual ground.
- Notices received under Art. 16 and their outcomes.
- Complaints under Art. 20 and outcomes.
- Automated means used: what, for what purpose, accuracy statements and
  safeguards.
- Art. 24(2): disaggregated data per above, published in a
  machine-readable, easily accessible form.

## Data lineage rule (binding)

Every figure in a published report records: source system, extract date,
extraction method, responsible person. Reports without lineage notes are
not published.

## Files

- `YYYY-H1-report.md` / `YYYY-H2-report.md` — published version (mirrored
  to the public channel decided by GC).
- `YYYY-Hx-data-lineage.md` — extraction notes (internal).
