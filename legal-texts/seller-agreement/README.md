# seller-agreement/ — Marketplace terms for traders

The P2B-critical text: professional users rely on it, and Art. 3(1) P2B
requires the mandatory information to be available in plain language here.

Mandatory P2B content checklist:
- [ ] grounds for suspension/termination/restriction (Art. 3(1)(a))
- [ ] additional distribution channels & affiliates (Art. 3(1)(b))
- [ ] other channels for goods/services of the same kind (Art. 3(1)(c))
- [ ] ranking parameters & their relative importance (Art. 5 — full spec
      in `platform-regulation/p2b/ranking-transparency.md`)
- [ ] ancillary goods/services rules (Art. 3(1)(e))
- [ ] data access description (Art. 9 — `platform-regulation/p2b/data-access.md`)
- [ ] complaint-handling + mediation (Art. 11/12 — `platform-regulation/dsa/complaint-handling.md`)

Commercial terms: commission structure, payout schedule & reserve rules,
listing rules, KYB obligations (DSA Art. 30), termination & delisting
mechanics. Commission changes are material — P2B notice applies.
