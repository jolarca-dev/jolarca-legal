# Seller Agreement — Journey of Life Marketplace

**Effective date:** [to be set upon publication]
**Last updated:** [to be set upon publication]

This Seller Agreement ("Agreement") is concluded between UAB "Journey of
Life" and each professional user who registers as a Seller on the
Marketplace. It supplements the Terms of Service and, in the event of
conflict between this Agreement and the Terms of Service on matters
specific to Sellers, this Agreement prevails.

## 1. Parties and onboarding (KYB)

### 1.1 Parties

- **Platform operator:** UAB "Journey of Life" (details in the Imprint).
- **Seller:** The natural or legal person who accepts this Agreement by
  completing the seller registration process on the Marketplace.

### 1.2 Trader verification (DSA Art. 30)

Before a Seller may list goods, JOL verifies the following information
from reliable sources:

| Requirement | Source |
|-------------|--------|
| Legal name and trading name | Trade register (JAR / UR / e-BR) |
| Address (registered office) | Trade register |
| Contact details (email, phone) | Self-declared; verified via confirmation |
| Trade register number | Trade register |
| VAT identification number | VIES validation |
| Self-certification of product safety compliance | Seller declaration |
| Identity of beneficial owner(s) | Trade register; AML threshold |

### 1.3 Verification outcomes

- **Verified:** Seller may list goods immediately.
- **Partially verified:** Seller may list goods with restricted
  categories or visibility until full verification is completed.
- **Failed verification:** Seller is notified of the deficiency and
  given 15 business days to remedy. Failure to remedy results in
  suspension.

### 1.4 Periodic re-verification

JOL re-verifies Seller information at least annually for active Sellers
and on an event-driven basis (payment failure, complaint spike, change
of ownership).

## 2. Listings and listing rules

### 2.1 Accuracy obligations

Sellers must ensure that all Listings are:

(a) Accurate and not misleading;
(b) Compliant with all applicable product safety legislation (including
GPSR Regulation (EU) 2023/988);
(c) Accompanied by clear images, descriptions, and pricing;
(d) Inclusive of all mandatory pre-contractual information required by
the Consumer Rights Directive (Art. 6).

### 2.2 Prohibited items

Sellers must not list items that are:

- Illegal under applicable law in any market where the Listing is
  visible;
- Counterfeit or infringing intellectual property rights;
- Protected cultural property or human remains;
- Weapons, ammunition, or explosives;
- Listed in the Community Standards as prohibited.

### 2.3 Sacred-goods sensitivity

Listings of religious, funerary, or memorial goods must comply with the
sacred-goods sensitivity rules in the Community Standards. This includes
respectful representation, prohibited framing (mockery, disparagement),
and category-specific imagery requirements.

### 2.4 GPSR compliance

Sellers, as economic operators, are responsible for:

- Ensuring products bear type/batch/serial identification;
- Including safety warnings in the language(s) of the target market;
- Designating an EU-established responsible person (Art. 4/16 GPSR)
  where required;
- Cooperating with JOL on Safety Gate notifications and recalls.

### 2.5 Listing review and removal

JOL reserves the right to restrict or remove Listings that violate this
Agreement, the Community Standards, or applicable law. Each restriction
or removal triggers a statement of reasons (DSA Art. 17) and the
Seller's right to appeal (Section 7).

## 3. Commissions and payouts

### 3.1 Commission structure

| Component | Rate | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|
| Transaction commission | [X]% of the net sale price (excl. VAT, shipping) | Charged per completed order |
| Payment processing fee | Passed through at cost from PSP | Itemised on monthly statement |
| Listing fee | None (currently) | Introduction of listing fees is a material change requiring 30-day notice |

The commission rate is set out in the Seller's onboarding schedule and
may be amended by JOL with 30 days' prior notice (P2B Art. 3(2)).

### 3.2 Payout schedule

- **Standard:** Payouts are processed weekly (every Monday) for the
  preceding week's completed orders, minus commissions and fees.
- **Minimum payout threshold:** €10.00. Balances below this threshold
  roll over to the next payout cycle.
- **Payout method:** SEPA bank transfer to the Seller's verified bank
  account.

### 3.3 Reserves

JOL may hold a reserve of up to [X]% of transaction proceeds where:

- The Seller's account is new (first 90 days);
- There is an elevated chargeback or complaint risk;
- The Seller is under investigation for policy violations.

Reserves are released within 30 days of the risk subsiding, with written
notice to the Seller.

### 3.4 Chargebacks and refunds

- Where a Buyer initiates a chargeback through their payment provider,
  the disputed amount is debited from the Seller's balance.
- Where JOL processes a refund (e.g., under consumer withdrawal rights),
  the refund amount and any associated commission reversal are applied
  to the Seller's account.
- Chargeback fees imposed by the PSP are passed through to the Seller.

### 3.5 Invoicing

JOL invoices Sellers monthly for commissions and fees. Invoices are
issued in accordance with the VAT and invoicing requirements applicable
in the Seller's jurisdiction. Sellers are responsible for their own tax
obligations.

## 4. Ranking

### 4.1 Main ranking parameters

Listings are ranked based on the following main parameters, listed in
order of relative importance:

| Rank | Parameter | Relative importance |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Relevance to search query | Highest |
| 2 | Listing completeness and quality | High |
| 3 | Seller performance (delivery speed, complaint rate) | High |
| 4 | Price competitiveness | Medium |
| 5 | Recency of listing | Medium |
| 6 | Buyer ratings and reviews | Medium |

### 4.2 No own-goods preference

JOL does not currently sell goods on the Marketplace. If JOL begins
offering its own goods or goods of affiliated sellers, the ranking
parameters and any differentiation will be disclosed transparently in
accordance with P2B Art. 5.

### 4.3 Paid placement

JOL does not currently offer paid placement or sponsored listings. If
introduced, this will be treated as a material change requiring 30-day
P2B notice and an update to this section and the ranking transparency
document.

### 4.4 Full transparency document

The complete ranking transparency specification is available at
`platform-regulation/p2b/ranking-transparency.md`.

## 5. Suspension, restriction, and termination

### 5.1 Grounds (P2B Art. 3(1)(a))

JOL may restrict, suspend, or terminate a Seller's account on the
following grounds:

(a) **Breach of this Agreement** — including violation of listing rules,
prohibited items, or accuracy obligations;
(b) **Breach of the Community Standards** — including sacred-goods
sensitivity rules;
(c) **Failure to maintain verified status** — including failure to
complete re-verification;
(d) **Legal obligation** — where required by law, court order, or
regulatory authority;
(e) **Safety risk** — where the Seller's goods pose a safety risk
(GPSR);
(f) **Inactivity** — no completed orders in 12 consecutive months;
(g) **Insolvency or bankruptcy** — of the Seller.

### 5.2 Notice and statement of reasons

Before taking a restriction, suspension, or termination action, JOL will:

1. Provide written notice to the Seller identifying the grounds and the
   specific provisions breached;
2. Provide a statement of reasons (DSA Art. 17) in plain language;
3. Allow a cure period of **15 business days** for remediable breaches
   (except where immediate action is necessary for safety, legal
   compliance, or to prevent fraud).

### 5.3 Appeals

Sellers may appeal any restriction, suspension, or termination decision
through the internal complaint-handling system (Section 7). The appeal
is reviewed by a human decision-maker. During the appeal process:

- Suspended Sellers' existing orders continue to be fulfilled;
- Funds in the Seller's account are preserved but not paid out until the
  appeal is resolved.

## 6. Data access

### 6.1 Data available to Sellers

Sellers have access to the following data through the Seller dashboard:

- Their own Listing data (including edit history);
- Order data for their own orders (buyer name, delivery address, items,
  amounts, status);
- Performance metrics (sales, delivery performance, complaint rates);
- Financial data (commissions, payouts, reserves).

### 6.2 Data export

Sellers may export their data at any time in a standard machine-readable
format (CSV). Available exports:

- Listings (current and historical);
- Orders (with buyer and financial details);
- Performance reports;
- Financial statements.

### 6.3 P2B Art. 9 compliance

The data-access description above satisfies the transparency obligation
under P2B Art. 9. Full details are available at
`platform-regulation/p2b/data-access.md`.

## 7. Complaints and mediation

### 7.1 Internal complaint handling

Sellers may submit complaints through the internal complaint-handling
system:

- **In-app:** Via the Seller dashboard "Help & Complaints" section.
- **Email:** [seller-complaints email].
- **Response time:** JOL aims to resolve standard complaints within 15
  business days.

The complaint system handles:

- Moderation decisions (listing removal, account restriction);
- Commission disputes;
- Ranking concerns;
- Technical issues affecting the Seller's account.

### 7.2 Mediation (P2B Art. 12)

If the internal complaint process does not resolve the dispute, the
Seller may refer the matter to a certified mediation body:

- **EU Mediation Register:** ec.europa.eu/p2b-mediation
- **Certified bodies:** Listed on the EU Commission's P2B mediation
  register.

Mediation is voluntary, non-binding (unless both parties agree), and
does not affect the Seller's right to bring legal proceedings.

## 8. Changes to this Agreement

### 8.1 Notice procedure

JOL may amend this Agreement. All changes are communicated to Sellers:

- **Material changes** (including commission rate changes, new fees,
  changes to ranking parameters): at least **30 days** before the
  effective date, via email and in-app notification.
- **Non-material changes** (clarifications, administrative updates): at
  least **15 days** before the effective date.

### 8.2 Right to terminate

If a Seller cannot reasonably be expected to accept a material change,
the Seller may terminate this Agreement before the change takes effect
without penalty. Termination in these circumstances follows the exit
procedure in Section 9.

### 8.3 Evidence channel

All change notices are archived and available to Sellers through the
Seller dashboard. The archive constitutes evidence of the notice for P2B
compliance purposes.

## 9. Term and exit

### 9.1 Term

This Agreement enters into force upon the Seller's acceptance during
registration and remains in effect until terminated by either party.

### 9.2 Termination by the Seller

The Seller may terminate this Agreement at any time with **30 days'**
written notice via the Seller dashboard or by email.

### 9.3 Termination by JOL

JOL may terminate this Agreement:

- With **60 days'** notice for convenience;
- Immediately for serious breaches (see Section 5.1).

### 9.4 Offboarding

Upon termination:

(a) **Listing removal:** All active Listings are delisted within 7 days.
(b) **Data export window:** The Seller has 30 days from termination to
export their data via the Seller dashboard.
(c) **Final payout:** Any remaining balance (net of outstanding
commissions, chargebacks, and reserves) is paid out within 30 days of
termination.
(d) **Record retention:** JOL retains Seller records as required by law
(tax, accounting, DSA accountability) per the retention schedule.

### 9.5 Survival

Sections 3 (commissions on pre-termination orders), 6 (data), 7
(complaints), and 9.4 (offboarding) survive termination.
