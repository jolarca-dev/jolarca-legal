## 3. How the Marketplace works

### 3.1 Listings

Sellers create Listings specifying the goods offered, price, description,
images, delivery options, and applicable return policy. JOL provides the
technical infrastructure for display and discovery but does not verify
the accuracy of Listing content unless notified.

Sellers are solely responsible for the accuracy, legality, and
completeness of their Listings.

### 3.2 Ordering and contract formation

1. A Buyer places an order by selecting a Listing and completing the
   checkout flow.
2. The order constitutes an offer to conclude a sales contract with the
   Seller.
3. The sales contract is formed when the Seller accepts the order
   (confirmation dispatch) or, where applicable, ships the goods.
4. JOL facilitates communication and payment but is not a party to the
   sales contract.

### 3.3 Payments

Payments are processed through JOL's designated payment service
provider(s) (PSP). JOL does not directly handle or store payment card
data. The PSP's terms and privacy policy apply to payment processing.

### 3.4 Delivery

Delivery is arranged by the Seller using the delivery options displayed
on the Listing. JOL may facilitate delivery through integrated carrier
partners (DPD, Omniva) but is not a party to the carriage contract
unless expressly stated.
