# buyer-terms/ — Consumer purchase terms

Consumer protection is mandatory law; these terms can only add rights,
never remove them (CRD 2011/83 as transposed in LT/LV/EE).

The load-bearing section is **withdrawal**:

- 14-day right of withdrawal for distance sales, with the model
  withdrawal form attached.
- **Exceptions must be handled with care** (CRD Art. 16): goods made to
  the consumer's specifications or clearly personalized — several of our
  product lines (engraved memorial items, bespoke sacred goods) qualify.
  The exception applies ONLY where the product genuinely qualifies;
  per-listing classification lives with the listing rules, and sacred
  items require case-by-case review before claiming the exception.
- Sealed goods unsuitable for return for health/hygiene reasons —
  assess per category, never blanket.
- Digital content delivery exceptions only with the Art. 16(m) consent
  flow implemented in product.

Other sections: delivery & risk transfer, payment, statutory warranty
(LT Civil Code / LV / EE equivalents — see glossary), returns procedure,
complaints & ODR platform link, digital-content conformity (Directive
(EU) 2019/770 where applicable).
