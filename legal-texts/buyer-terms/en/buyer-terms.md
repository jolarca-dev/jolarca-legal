# Buyer Terms — Journey of Life Marketplace

**Effective date:** [to be set upon publication]
**Last updated:** [to be set upon publication]

These Buyer Terms govern purchases made by consumers on the Journey of
Life Marketplace. They supplement the Terms of Service. In the event of
conflict, these Buyer Terms prevail for matters specific to Buyers.

**Mandatory consumer rights** under EU and national law (Directive
2011/83/EU, national transpositions) can only be added to, never
waived. Where these terms refer to statutory rights, the statutory
provision prevails if it provides greater protection.

## 1. Ordering and contract formation

### 1.1 Parties to the sales contract

When you purchase goods on the Marketplace, the sales contract is
concluded directly between you (the Buyer) and the Seller identified on
the Listing. Journey of Life (JOL) operates the Marketplace as an
intermediary and is **not** a party to the sales contract unless
expressly stated.

### 1.2 How to place an order

1. Browse or search for goods on the Marketplace.
2. Select a Listing and review the product details, price, delivery
   costs, and Seller information.
3. Add the item to your cart and proceed to checkout.
4. Enter your delivery address and select a delivery method.
5. Review the order summary (including total price, VAT, delivery
   costs).
6. Confirm the order and complete payment.

### 1.3 When the contract forms

The sales contract is formed when you receive the **order confirmation**
from the Seller (dispatched via email or in-app notification). The
confirmation is sent after the Seller accepts your order or ships the
goods, whichever occurs first.

### 1.4 Pre-contractual information

Before you are bound by any order, the Marketplace displays all
mandatory pre-contractual information required by Art. 6 of the Consumer
Rights Directive (2011/83/EU), including:

- Main characteristics of the goods;
- Total price including taxes and delivery costs;
- Seller identity and contact details;
- Delivery arrangements and timeline;
- Your right of withdrawal and the model withdrawal form;
- Applicable complaint-handling policy.

## 2. Prices, VAT, and payment

### 2.1 Price display

All prices displayed on Listings include applicable VAT. Delivery costs
are displayed separately and added to the total at checkout. The total
price (inclusive of VAT and delivery) is clearly shown before you
confirm your order.

### 2.2 VAT and OSS

- For Buyers in Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia, VAT is charged at the
  rate applicable in the Buyer's country of residence.
- Where the Seller is established in a different EU Member State, VAT
  may be accounted for through the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) mechanism.
  This does not affect the price you pay.

### 2.3 Payment methods

Payments are processed through JOL's designated payment service provider
(Stripe). Accepted methods include:

- Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard);
- Bank transfer (where available);
- Other methods as displayed at checkout.

JOL does not store your full payment card details.

## 3. Delivery and risk transfer

### 3.1 Delivery timeline

Sellers dispatch goods within the timeframe stated on the Listing
(typically 1–5 business days). Delivery times via carrier (DPD, Omniva)
are additional and depend on the selected method:

| Method | Typical delivery time |
|--------|----------------------|
| Parcel locker (DPD/Omniva) | 2–4 business days from dispatch |
| Courier delivery | 2–5 business days from dispatch |
| Standard post | 5–10 business days from dispatch |

### 3.2 Risk transfer

For consumer Buyers, the risk of loss or damage to the goods passes to
you only when you (or a third party you designate, other than the
carrier) take physical possession of the goods.

### 3.3 Non-delivery

If goods are not delivered within 30 days of the order confirmation (or
the agreed delivery date), you may:

1. Contact the Seller via the Marketplace messaging system;
2. Request JOL's assistance in resolving the matter;
3. Cancel the order and receive a full refund.

## 4. Right of withdrawal (14 days)

### 4.1 Your statutory right

You have the right to withdraw from the sales contract within **14
calendar days** of receiving the goods, without giving any reason and
without incurring any costs (except as stated in Section 5).

### 4.2 Withdrawal period

The 14-day withdrawal period begins:

- For a single item: on the day you (or your nominated third party)
  receive the goods.
- For multiple items in one order: on the day you receive the last item.
- For regular deliveries: on the day you receive the first item.

### 4.3 How to withdraw

To exercise your right of withdrawal, notify the Seller of your decision
by:

- **In-app:** Via the "My Orders" section → "Return / Withdraw" button;
- **Email:** To the Seller's contact address shown on the order
  confirmation;
- **Model form:** Using the model withdrawal form below (Annex A).

You may also notify JOL, and we will forward your withdrawal to the
Seller.

### 4.4 Effect of withdrawal

- You must return the goods to the Seller within **14 days** of
  notifying your withdrawal.
- The Seller must refund all payments (including standard delivery
  costs) within **14 days** of receiving the returned goods or evidence
  that you have sent them back.
- Refunds are made using the same payment method you used, unless you
  expressly agree otherwise.

### 4.5 Condition of returned goods

You may examine the goods as you would in a physical store. You are
liable for any diminished value of the goods resulting from handling
beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics, and
functioning of the goods.

## 5. Withdrawal exceptions

The right of withdrawal does **not** apply in the following cases, as
per Art. 16 of the Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU):

### 5.1 Personalised or custom-made goods

Goods made to the consumer's specifications or clearly personalised —
for example:

- Engraved memorial items (headstones, plaques, urns with inscriptions);
- Bespoke sacred goods (custom religious artwork, personalised memorial
  jewellery);
- Any Listing marked as "custom-made" or "personalised" at the point of
  purchase.

**Important:** This exception applies ONLY where the product is
genuinely made to your specifications or clearly personalised. The
classification is made per listing, and you will be clearly informed
before purchase that the withdrawal right does not apply.

### 5.2 Sealed goods unsuitable for return (hygiene)

Sealed goods that are not suitable for return for health or hygiene
reasons, where the seal has been broken after delivery — for example:

- Sealed cosmetic or care products associated with funeral/memorial
  services;
- Sealed food or beverage items (if offered).

This exception applies ONLY where the seal was intact at delivery and
has been broken by you.

### 5.3 Digital content

Digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, where performance has
begun with your prior express consent and acknowledgement that you
thereby lose your withdrawal right. This applies only where the
Marketplace offers digital content (e.g., downloadable memorial
templates) and the consent flow has been properly implemented.

### 5.4 Other statutory exceptions

Additional exceptions under Art. 16 CRD may apply where relevant
(including goods that become inseparably mixed with other items after
delivery, alcoholic beverages whose price depends on market fluctuations).

## 6. Warranty and conformity

### 6.1 Statutory warranty

You have a **statutory warranty** (įstatyminė kokybės garantija /
likumiskā garantija / seadusjärgne garantii) under EU and national law.
The Seller must deliver goods that:

- Match the description and sample/model shown;
- Are fit for any particular purpose you informed the Seller of;
- Are of satisfactory quality;
- Conform with the goods over a period of time.

### 6.2 Conformity period

Under Directive (EU) 2019/771, the Seller is liable for any lack of
conformity that becomes apparent within **2 years** of delivery. In
Lithuania, the burden of proof reverses to the Seller after 6 months
(for the first year under the updated LT transposition).

### 6.3 Your remedies

Where goods do not conform, you may require the Seller to:

1. **Repair** the goods; or
2. **Replace** the goods;
3. If neither is possible or timely, a **price reduction** or
   **termination** of the contract (full refund).

### 6.4 Who to contact

Contact the **Seller** first (via the Marketplace messaging system). If
the Seller does not respond or resolve the issue, JOL will facilitate
the complaint (see Section 8).

## 7. Returns procedure

### 7.1 Steps to return

1. Notify the Seller of your withdrawal or conformity claim (Section 4
   or Section 6).
2. Receive return instructions from the Seller (including the return
   address).
3. Package the goods securely and ship to the return address.
4. Retain proof of postage (tracking number).
5. The Seller processes the refund within 14 days of receiving the
   goods.

### 7.2 Return costs

- **Withdrawal (14-day right):** You bear the direct cost of returning
  the goods unless the Seller agreed to bear it or failed to inform you
  that you must bear it.
- **Non-conformity (warranty):** The Seller bears all return costs.

### 7.3 Condition requirements

Returned goods should include all original packaging, accessories, and
documentation. Goods may be handled as necessary to inspect them but
should not be used beyond what is reasonable.

## 8. Complaints and dispute resolution

### 8.1 Seller first

For any issue with your order, contact the Seller directly via the
Marketplace messaging system. Most issues are resolved at this stage.

### 8.2 Marketplace facilitation

If the Seller does not resolve your complaint, JOL will facilitate:

- Contact the Seller on your behalf;
- Review the evidence (messages, photos, order details);
- Issue a recommendation to the Seller.

### 8.3 Online Dispute Resolution (ODR)

If the issue remains unresolved, you may use the **EU Online Dispute
Resolution platform**:

**https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr**

The ODR platform accepts complaints from consumers in EU Member States
relating to online purchases.
