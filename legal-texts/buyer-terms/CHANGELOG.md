## v1.0.0 — [effective date TBD] (initial publication)

- Initial publication of the Buyer Terms for the Journey of Life
  Marketplace.
- Covers: ordering and contract formation (buyer-seller direct
  contract, marketplace as intermediary), prices/VAT/payment (OSS
  context), delivery and risk transfer (carrier handoff, risk passes on
  possession), 14-day right of withdrawal (exercise mechanics, model
  withdrawal form as Annex A), withdrawal exceptions (CRD Art. 16:
  personalised/custom goods, sealed hygiene goods, digital content),
  warranty and conformity (statutory warranty, 2-year conformity
  period, remedies), returns procedure, complaints and dispute
  resolution (ODR platform link, national authorities), contact.
- Who is affected: all buyers (consumers).
- This is the first published version; no prior version to supersede.
