### OSS note

JOL is registered for the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) VAT scheme via VMI
(Lithuania). Under the OSS scheme, JOL accounts for VAT on qualifying
cross-border B2C distance sales to consumers in other EU Member States.
This means that for qualifying transactions, the VAT you pay is
remitted by JOL to the tax authority of your Member State via the OSS
portal. The OSS registration does not change the price you see — VAT is
always included in the displayed price.

## Per-country legal disclosures

### Lithuania

JOL is registered in the Republic of Lithuania. The Marketplace complies
with the Law on Electronic Commerce of the Republic of Lithuania
(Elektroninės komercijos įstatymas) and the Civil Code of the Republic
of Lithuania.

### Latvia

The Marketplace operates in Latvia in compliance with the Consumer
Rights Protection Law (Patērētāju tiesību aizsardzības likums) and the
Electronic Commerce Law (Elektroniskā komercija). Information duties are
fulfilled in this Imprint and in the Marketplace's Latvian-language
interface.

### Estonia

The Marketplace operates in Estonia in compliance with the Estonian
Consumer Protection Act (Tarbijakaitseseadus) and the Information
Society Services Act (Infoühiskonna teenuse seadus). Information duties
are fulfilled in this Imprint and in the Marketplace's Estonian-language
interface.
