## 4. Marketplace-specific flows

### 4.1 Payment processing

Payments are processed by our PSP (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd.). When you
complete a purchase:

- The PSP receives the transaction amount, Buyer email, and payment
  instrument details.
- JOL receives a transaction confirmation token and status.
- The PSP's own privacy policy governs their processing.

For Sellers, the PSP receives bank account details for payout processing.

### 4.2 Delivery partners

When a Buyer places an order, the Seller (or JOL on the Seller's behalf)
shares the Buyer's name, delivery address, and contact details with the
selected carrier (DPD, Omniva, or other) solely for delivery purposes.

### 4.3 Reviews

Buyers may voluntarily leave reviews. Review content (including the
display name) is visible to other Users. Reviews are not edited by JOL
except for removal of illegal content following a notice-and-action
procedure.

### 4.4 KYB / trader verification

For professional users, JOL collects and verifies identity and business
data as required by DSA Art. 30. Verification records are retained per
the retention schedule (relationship + 5 years).

## 5. AI-assisted features

### 5.1 Catalog translation

JOL uses AI-assisted translation to help Sellers reach buyers across
LT/LV/EE language markets. The following data may be processed:

- Listing titles and descriptions (product text only).
- **No personal data** (names, addresses, contact details) is sent to
  the AI provider. A PII guardrail filters personal data before
  processing.

### 5.2 Search and recommendation

JOL uses algorithmic ranking to display search results and
recommendations. The main ranking parameters are described in the Seller
Agreement (Section 4) and the ranking transparency document
(`platform-regulation/p2b/ranking-transparency.md`).

### 5.3 Human oversight and consequences

- AI-generated translations are reviewed before publication where
  technically feasible.
- Algorithmic ranking does not involve solely automated decision-making
  that produces legal or similarly significant effects (no Art. 22
  profiling).
- Users may object to AI-assisted processing based on legitimate
  interest by contacting the DPO.

## 6. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

### 6.1 General principle

JOL does not transfer personal data to countries outside the EU/EEA
except where necessary for service provision and only with appropriate
safeguards.

### 6.2 Mechanisms

Where transfers occur (e.g., to sub-processors using cloud
infrastructure outside the EU/EEA), JOL relies on:

- **Adequacy decisions** (Art. 45 GDPR) where available; or
- **Standard Contractual Clauses** (Commission Implementing Regulation
  2021/914) with the appropriate module, supplemented by a Transfer
  Impact Assessment (TIA) where required.

### 6.3 Specific transfers

| Recipient | Purpose | Mechanism |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Stripe (US) | Payment processing | EU-US Data Privacy Framework / SCCs |
| AI providers | Catalog translation | SCCs + TIA; PII filtered pre-transfer |
| Cloud hosting | Infrastructure | SCCs; EU data residency where possible |

A copy of the safeguards is available from the DPO on request.

## 7. Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes
described in Section 3, or as required by law.

| Data category | Retention period | Legal basis |
|---------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Account data (Buyers) | Duration of account + 30 days after deletion request | Contract performance |
| Order records | 10 years from transaction date | Tax and accounting obligations |
| Seller verification (KYB) | Relationship duration + 5 years | DSA Art. 30 accountability |
| Payment records | 10 years from transaction date | Tax obligations |
| Cookie consent records | 24 months from consent | ePrivacy / accountability |
| Support / complaint records | 5 years from last interaction | Legitimate interest; limitation |
| Marketing consent records | Duration of consent + 5 years | Accountability |
| Analytics data (pseudonymised) | 24 months | Legitimate interest |
| Server / security logs | 90 days | Legitimate interest (security) |

After the retention period expires, data is securely deleted or
anonymised.

## 8. Your rights

### 8.1 Overview of rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

| Right | Description | GDPR Article |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| Access | Obtain a copy of your personal data and information about processing | Art. 15 |
| Rectification | Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data | Art. 16 |
| Erasure ("right to be forgotten") | Delete your personal data in certain circumstances | Art. 17 |
| Restriction | Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances | Art. 18 |
| Portability | Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format | Art. 20 |
| Object | Object to processing based on legitimate interest or for direct marketing | Art. 21 |
| Withdraw consent | Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent | Art. 7(3) |

### 8.2 How to exercise your rights

- **Online:** Through your account settings (for access, rectification,
  account deletion).
- **Email:** Contact the DPO at dpo@journeyoflife.org.
- **Postal:** [DPO postal address].

We will respond to your request within **30 days** of receipt. In complex
cases, this period may be extended by up to 60 days, with notice to you.

### 8.3 Limitations

The right to erasure does not apply where processing is necessary for:

- Compliance with a legal obligation (e.g., tax records);
- The establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims;
- Freedom of expression and information.
