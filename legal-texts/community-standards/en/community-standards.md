# Community Standards — Journey of Life Marketplace

**Effective date:** [to be set upon publication]
**Last updated:** [to be set upon publication]

These Community Standards define the rules of conduct for all Users of
the Journey of Life Marketplace. They form the contractual basis for
content moderation under the Digital Services Act (DSA) Art. 14 and are
referenced by the Terms of Service and Seller Agreement.

## 1. Prohibited items

The following items are **prohibited** from listing or sale on the
Marketplace. Listings containing these items will be removed and may
result in account suspension or termination.

### 1.1 Illegal goods

- Goods whose sale or possession is illegal under Lithuanian, Latvian,
  Estonian, or EU law;
- Narcotics and controlled substances;
- Stolen property.

### 1.2 Counterfeit goods

- Goods that infringe trademarks, copyrights, design rights, or other
  intellectual property;
- Goods bearing false indications of origin or authenticity;
- "Replica" or "inspired by" goods that create confusion with
  protected brands.

### 1.3 Protected cultural property

- Cultural artefacts, archaeological items, or protected heritage
  objects;
- Items subject to export restrictions under national cultural heritage
  laws;
- Human remains or body parts (except where expressly permitted for
  memorial/funerary purposes with proper documentation).

### 1.4 Weapons and dangerous items

- Firearms, ammunition, and explosives;
- Knives marketed as weapons (excluding kitchen, craft, or ceremonial
  knives);
- Self-defence sprays, stun guns, or similar devices where restricted
  by national law.

### 1.5 Other prohibited categories

- Tobacco products and e-cigarettes;
- Prescription medicines;
- Live animals;
- Pornographic or sexually explicit material;
- Gambling equipment (where restricted by law);
- Items promoting hate, violence, or discrimination.

### 1.6 Market-specific restrictions

Certain items may be legal in one market but prohibited in another.
Sellers are responsible for ensuring their Listings comply with the laws
of every market where the Listing is visible. JOL may restrict Listings
to specific markets where necessary.

## 2. Sacred-goods sensitivity rules

The Marketplace specialises in goods with religious, spiritual,
funerary, and memorial significance. These categories require
particular care.

### 2.1 Respectful representation

All Listings of sacred goods (religious items, funerary products,
memorial goods) must:

- Represent the item accurately and respectfully;
- Use appropriate imagery that reflects the item's purpose and
  significance;
- Include accurate descriptions of religious or cultural associations
  where relevant.

### 2.2 Prohibited framing

The following are prohibited:

- Mockery, disparagement, or trivialisation of sacred goods;
- Use of sacred imagery in contexts unrelated to the item's purpose;
- Misrepresentation of religious affiliation or significance;
- Comparative or competitive framing that disparages other traditions.

### 2.3 Category imagery rules

| Category | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Religious icons and artwork | Must not be altered, defaced, or used decoratively out of context |
| Funerary and memorial goods | Must be presented with dignity; no promotional discounting visible in primary imagery |
| Candles and incense (religious) | Must include fire-safety warnings; must not misrepresent religious significance |
| Sacred texts | Must be presented respectfully; accurate edition/translation information required |

### 2.4 Review triggers

Listings in sacred-goods categories are subject to enhanced review:

- First listing in a new sacred-goods subcategory: manual review before
  publication;
- Complaints from Users or religious communities: expedited review;
- Seasonal sensitivity periods (e.g., major religious holidays,
  memorial days): heightened monitoring.

## 3. Listing accuracy and counterfeit policy

### 3.1 Accuracy requirements

All Listings must:

- Accurately describe the goods being sold;
- Include clear, original photographs (or clearly marked stock images
  with permission);
- State the correct price, condition (new/used/refurbished), and
  quantity available;
- Include all mandatory pre-contractual information (Consumer Rights
  Directive Art. 6);
- State the country of origin or manufacture where required by law.

### 3.2 GPSR responsible person

In accordance with the General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988:

- Sellers must ensure products have type, batch, or serial
  identification;
- An EU-established responsible person must be designated for products
  placed on the EU market;
- Safety warnings must be provided in the language(s) of the target
  market;
- The responsible person's contact details must appear in the Listing.

### 3.3 Authenticity claims

Where a Seller represents goods as authentic, branded, or from a
specific origin:

- JOL may request evidence of authenticity (purchase receipts,
  distributor certificates, brand authorisation letters);
- Failure to provide evidence within 10 business days results in
  Listing removal;
- Repeated authenticity claims without evidence may result in account
  suspension.

## 4. Reviews and social proof

### 4.1 Authenticity

- Only Buyers who have completed a purchase through the Marketplace may
  leave reviews for that transaction.
- Incentivised reviews (payment, discounts, free goods in exchange for
  positive reviews) are prohibited.
- Fake reviews, review manipulation, or review-rings are prohibited and
  result in immediate account suspension.

### 4.2 Content standards for reviews

Reviews must:

- Relate to the purchased product and the Buyer's genuine experience;
- Not contain defamatory, threatening, or abusive language;
- Not disclose personal information about the Seller or third parties;
- Not contain promotional content or links to external sites.

### 4.3 Moderation of reviews

JOL may remove reviews that:

- Violate these content standards;
- Are reported by Users as inappropriate;
- Are found to be inauthentic or manipulated.

Removal triggers a statement of reasons and the right to appeal.

## 5. Enforcement ladder

JOL applies a graduated enforcement approach. The severity and
frequency of violations determine the response.

### 5.1 Enforcement steps

| Step | Action | When applied |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | **Warning** | First minor violation; educational notice |
| 2 | **Content restriction** | Repeated minor violations or single moderate violation; Listing visibility reduced or content hidden |
| 3 | **Account suspension** | Serious violation, repeated violations after warnings, or failure to remedy |
| 4 | **Account termination** | Severe violation, persistent non-compliance, illegal activity |

### 5.2 Proportionality

JOL selects the least restrictive measure proportionate to:

- The severity of the violation;
- The frequency of violations by the same User;
- The harm caused or likely to be caused;
- Whether the violation was intentional or negligent.

### 5.3 Immediate action

Steps 3 and 4 may be applied without prior warning where:

- The content is illegal or poses a safety risk;
- Immediate action is necessary to protect Users or third parties;
- Required by law or regulatory authority.

### 5.4 Notice and appeal

Every enforcement action is accompanied by:

- A statement of reasons identifying the action, grounds, and provision
  breached (DSA Art. 17);
- Information on how to appeal via the internal complaint-handling
  system (DSA Art. 20, P2B Art. 11/12).

## 6. Reporting illegal content

### 6.1 How to report

Anyone may report content they consider illegal on the Marketplace:

- **In-app:** Click the "Report" button on any Listing, review, or
  profile page;
- **Email:** [reporting email];
- **No login required:** Reporting is available without an account.

### 6.2 What to include

Reports should contain (per DSA Art. 16(2)):

- A substantiated explanation of why the content is illegal;
- The exact location (URL or Listing ID);
- Your name and email address (for follow-up);
- A statement of good faith.

### 6.3 Trusted flaggers

Notices from qualified trusted flaggers (Art. 22 DSA, when designated)
receive priority processing and are treated as non-abusive.

### 6.4 Processing of reports

1. Acknowledgement of receipt without undue delay;
2. Triage and assessment;
3. Decision (action or no action);
4. Statement of reasons to the notifier and affected User;
5. Appeal rights communicated.

### 6.5 Abuse of reporting

Abusive or bad-faith reporting (e.g., mass reporting to suppress a
competitor) is itself a violation and may result in enforcement action
against the reporter.

## 7. Changes to these standards

JOL may update these Community Standards to reflect:

- Changes in applicable law or regulatory guidance;
- New product categories or market conditions;
- Lessons from enforcement experience.

Changes are communicated to all Users via email and/or in-app
notification. Material changes (new prohibited categories, changes to
enforcement procedures) follow the notice procedure in the Terms of
Service, including the P2B 30-day notice for professional users.
