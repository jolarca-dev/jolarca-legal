## v1.0.0 — [effective date TBD] (initial publication)

- Initial publication of the Community Standards for the Journey of Life
  Marketplace.
- Covers: prohibited items (illegal goods, counterfeit, cultural
  property, weapons, other categories, market-specific restrictions),
  sacred-goods sensitivity rules (respectful representation, prohibited
  framing, category imagery rules, review triggers), listing accuracy
  and counterfeit policy (GPSR responsible person, authenticity
  claims), reviews and social proof (authenticity, content standards,
  moderation), enforcement ladder (warning → restriction → suspension →
  termination, proportionality, immediate action, notice and appeal),
  reporting illegal content (how to report, trusted flaggers,
  processing flow, abuse handling), changes procedure.
- Who is affected: all users.
- This is the first published version; no prior version to supersede.
