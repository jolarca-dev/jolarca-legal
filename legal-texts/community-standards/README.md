# community-standards/ — Prohibited items, moderation basis, sacred-goods rules

This text is the contractual basis for moderation decisions, so it must be
drafted to DSA Art. 14 standards: clear, specific, and machine-checkable
enough that a statement-of-reasons (Art. 17) can quote it.

Sections:

1. Prohibited items — illegal goods, counterfeit, weapons, human remains,
   protected cultural property (check LT/LV/EE export rules).
2. **Sacred-goods sensitivity rules** — the differentiator of this
   marketplace: respectful representation requirements for religious and
   funerary items; no mockery/disparagement framing; category-specific
   imagery rules.
3. Listing accuracy & counterfeit policy (ties to GPSR responsible person).
4. Content rules for reviews (Omnibus: authenticity; no paid fake reviews).
5. Enforcement ladder: warn → restrict → suspend → terminate, each step
   mapped to the DSA complaint/appeal rights.
6. Reporting illegal content (pointer to the Art. 16 flow spec).

Changes here are effectively changes to our moderation powers — treat as
MAJOR for DSA-exposed sections; archive notice evidence.
